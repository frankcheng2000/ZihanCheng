Biography
======
Zihan Cheng received the B.Eng. degree in Software Engineering from University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), Chengdu, China, in 2022, where he is currently pursuing the M.S. degree in Software Engineering. His research interests include model compression, knowledge distillation, and image super-resolution. 
He has authored peer-reviewed publications in IEEE International Conference on Multimedia & Expo 2025 and IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing 2026.
Mr. Cheng serves as a reviewer for several conferences. Contact him via GitHub (https://github.com/frankcheng2000) or zihancheng@std.uestc.edu.cn.
